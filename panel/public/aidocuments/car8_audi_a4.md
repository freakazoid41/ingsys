A premium compact executive sedan that offers Audi's quattro all-wheel drive, advanced technology, and sophisticated design.

## Detailed Specifications

The Audi A4 features a turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine that delivers refined performance and efficiency. The engine is paired with a seven-speed S tronic dual-clutch automatic transmission that provides quick and smooth shifts. The vehicle's quattro all-wheel-drive system provides excellent traction and handling.

## Safety Features

The A4 includes Audi's comprehensive safety systems, featuring Audi pre sense, lane departure warning, adaptive cruise control, and traffic sign recognition. The vehicle features a rigid body structure and multiple airbags for occupant protection.

## Interior and Comfort

The A4's interior combines luxury with technology, featuring premium materials, ambient lighting, and the latest Audi virtual cockpit. The cabin offers excellent ergonomics with available massage seats and advanced climate control. Cargo space is 16 cubic feet, suitable for daily use.

## Performance and Handling

The A4 delivers composed and engaging driving dynamics with precise steering and adaptive suspension. The available S4 variant offers significantly more performance with a 3.0-liter V6 turbo engine producing 349 horsepower. Audi's quattro system ensures excellent grip in all conditions.

## History and Legacy

Since its introduction in 1994, the A4 has been Audi's best-selling model, representing the brand's core values of technology and luxury. The B9 generation (2016-present) has evolved with each facelift to include the latest technology and design refinements.
