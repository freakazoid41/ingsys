## Detailed Specifications

The Altima's naturally aspirated engine provides smooth power, enhanced by Nissan's CVT for optimal efficiency. Its spacious interior makes it family-friendly.

## Safety Features

Nissan Safety Shield 360 includes intelligent forward collision warning and blind-spot warning, ensuring comprehensive protection.

## Interior and Comfort

Comfortable seating and NissanConnect infotainment with navigation. Quiet cabin for long trips.

## Performance and Handling

Balanced ride with responsive steering. The SR trim adds sporty elements.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 1992, the Altima has evolved to offer modern features while maintaining affordability.
