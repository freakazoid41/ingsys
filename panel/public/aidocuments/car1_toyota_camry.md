## Detailed Specifications

The Toyota Camry is equipped with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine that delivers smooth performance and excellent fuel efficiency. The engine is paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission that provides seamless shifting and responsive acceleration. The vehicle's fuel economy ratings are impressive, achieving up to 39 miles per gallon on the highway, making it an economical choice for daily commuting.

## Safety Features

Safety is a top priority in the Camry, featuring Toyota Safety Sense 2.0, which includes adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assist, automatic emergency braking, and pedestrian detection. The vehicle also comes with a comprehensive suite of airbags, stability control, and advanced driver assistance systems to ensure the safety of occupants.

## Interior and Comfort

The interior of the Camry offers a spacious cabin with high-quality materials and ergonomic design. Seats are comfortable for long drives, and the infotainment system includes a large touchscreen display with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto integration. Cargo space is adequate for a sedan, with 15.1 cubic feet of trunk space.

## Performance and Handling

Despite being a family sedan, the Camry offers engaging driving dynamics with precise steering and a smooth suspension. The hybrid variant provides even better efficiency without compromising on power. Overall, the Toyota Camry remains a benchmark in the midsize sedan segment for reliability and value.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 1982, the Camry has evolved over generations to become one of the best-selling cars in the world. Its reputation for durability and low maintenance costs has made it a favorite among consumers. The 2023 model continues this legacy with modern technology and refined design.
