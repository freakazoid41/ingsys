A comfortable and spacious midsize sedan that offers Nissan Intelligent Mobility features and family-friendly design.

## Detailed Specifications

The Nissan Altima is equipped with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine that provides smooth and efficient performance. The engine is paired with a continuously variable transmission (CVT) that optimizes fuel efficiency. The vehicle's extended wheelbase provides excellent interior space and ride comfort.

## Safety Features

The Altima includes Nissan Safety Shield 360, featuring automatic emergency braking, blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, and lane-keeping assist. The vehicle has earned top safety ratings and includes a comprehensive airbag system for occupant protection.

## Interior and Comfort

The Altima's interior offers a spacious and comfortable cabin with soft-touch materials and modern design. The available zero-gravity seats provide excellent comfort for long drives. The infotainment system features an 8-inch touchscreen with NissanConnect services and wireless smartphone integration.

## Performance and Handling

The Altima delivers a smooth and composed ride quality suitable for family transportation. The suspension is tuned for comfort while maintaining responsive handling. The available 2.0-liter turbocharged engine in SR models provides more spirited performance.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 1993, the Altima has become one of Nissan's most successful models in North America. The sixth-generation Altima (introduced in 2019) features modern styling and technology while maintaining the model's reputation for reliability and value.

## Variants

- S: Base model with standard features.
- SV: Mid-level trim with enhanced comfort.
- SR: Sporty variant with unique styling.
- Platinum: Luxury trim with premium features.
- SR/Platinum Reserve: Special edition models.
