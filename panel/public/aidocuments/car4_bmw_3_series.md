## Detailed Specifications

The 3 Series boasts a twin-turbocharged inline-four engine delivering smooth yet potent power. Its sophisticated suspension and precise steering define the ultimate driving machine ethos.

## Safety Features

BMW's Driving Assistance Professional package includes adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assist, and pedestrian detection, ensuring top-tier safety.

## Interior and Comfort

Premium leather upholstery and advanced iDrive system provide a refined cabin. Wireless charging and ambient lighting add to the luxury.

## Performance and Handling

Agile chassis with optional M Sport suspension for enhanced dynamics. The M340i variant offers thrilling acceleration.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 1975, the 3 Series has set standards for compact executive cars, evolving with cutting-edge technology.

## Variants

- 330i: Balanced performance.
- 330e: Plug-in hybrid efficiency.
- M340i: High-performance model.
- 330d: Diesel option for Europe.
