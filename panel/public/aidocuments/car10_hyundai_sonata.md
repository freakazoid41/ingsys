A sophisticated midsize sedan that offers Hyundai's modern design, advanced technology, and excellent warranty coverage.

## Detailed Specifications

The Hyundai Sonata features a 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine that delivers smooth and efficient performance. The engine is paired with an eight-speed automatic transmission that provides responsive acceleration. The vehicle's platform offers excellent interior space and refined driving dynamics.

## Safety Features

The Sonata includes Hyundai Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go, forward collision avoidance assist, lane-keeping assist, and blind-spot collision avoidance. The vehicle has earned top safety ratings from NHTSA and IIHS, with comprehensive occupant protection systems.

## Interior and Comfort

The Sonata's interior features premium materials and modern design with dual 12.3-inch displays for the instrument cluster and infotainment system. The cabin offers excellent comfort with available heated and ventilated seats. Cargo space is 16.3 cubic feet, among the best in its class.

## Performance and Handling

The Sonata provides a comfortable and refined driving experience with well-tuned suspension and precise steering. The available turbocharged engine in N Line models offers more engaging performance. Hyundai's Smart Cruise Control enhances long-distance driving comfort.

## History and Legacy

Since its introduction in 1985, the Sonata has evolved from a budget option to a sophisticated midsize sedan. The eighth-generation Sonata (introduced in 2020) features bold styling and advanced technology while maintaining Hyundai's reputation for reliability and value.

## Variants

- SE: Base model with standard features.
- SEL: Enhanced comfort and technology.
- Limited: Premium features and styling.
- N Line: Sporty variant with performance enhancements.
- Hybrid: Eco-friendly powertrain option.
