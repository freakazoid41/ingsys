A luxury compact executive sedan that offers Mercedes-Benz's signature comfort, technology, and refinement.

## Detailed Specifications

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class features a turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine that provides smooth and responsive performance. The engine is paired with a nine-speed automatic transmission that offers seamless shifting. The vehicle's air suspension system provides excellent ride comfort while maintaining composed handling.

## Safety Features

The C-Class includes Mercedes-Benz's comprehensive safety suite, featuring active brake assist, active lane-keeping assist, active blind-spot assist, and PRE-SAFE occupant protection system. The vehicle has earned top safety ratings and includes advanced driver assistance features for enhanced security.

## Interior and Comfort

The interior of the C-Class exemplifies luxury with premium materials, ambient lighting, and ergonomic design. The cabin features the latest MBUX infotainment system with natural voice control, wireless charging, and a large touchscreen display. Rear seat comfort is excellent, and cargo space is 12.6 cubic feet.

## Performance and Handling

The C-Class offers engaging driving dynamics with precise steering and adaptive suspension. The available 4MATIC all-wheel-drive system provides excellent traction in various conditions. AMG variants offer significantly more performance with engines up to 4.0-liter V8 producing over 400 horsepower.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 1993, the C-Class has become Mercedes-Benz's best-selling model worldwide. Each generation has introduced innovative technology and luxury features. The W206 generation (2022-present) continues this tradition with advanced digital features and autonomous driving capabilities.

## Variants

- C 300: Base model with 2.0L turbo engine.
- C 300 4MATIC: All-wheel-drive variant.
- AMG C 43: Performance model with 3.0L V6.
- AMG C 63: High-performance variant with 4.0L V8.
- C 300 e: Plug-in hybrid option.
