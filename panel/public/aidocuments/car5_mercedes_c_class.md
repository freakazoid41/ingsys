A sophisticated compact luxury sedan offering comfort, technology, and prestige.

## Detailed Specifications

Equipped with a turbocharged four-cylinder engine, the C-Class provides refined power delivery. The 9-speed automatic transmission ensures seamless shifts, complemented by air suspension for a plush ride.

## Safety Features

Mercedes-Benz Intelligent Drive includes active brake assist, blind-spot assist, and 360-degree camera, prioritizing occupant safety.

## Interior and Comfort

Opulent cabin with Nappa leather and Burmester sound system. The MBUX infotainment is intuitive and feature-rich.

## Performance and Handling

Balanced handling with four-wheel drive options. The AMG variants deliver sporty performance.

## History and Legacy

Since 1993, the C-Class has represented Mercedes' entry into luxury, blending innovation with tradition.

## Variants

- C 300: Standard luxury.
- C 300 4MATIC: All-wheel drive.
- AMG C 43: Performance-oriented.
- C 300 e: Hybrid efficiency.

This model showcases Mercedes' commitment to luxury and innovation.
