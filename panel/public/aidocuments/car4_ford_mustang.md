An American muscle car icon that combines performance, style, and modern technology in a legendary package.

## Detailed Specifications

The Ford Mustang is available with several engine options, including the 2.3-liter EcoBoost four-cylinder that delivers spirited performance with 315 horsepower. The engine is paired with either a six-speed manual or ten-speed automatic transmission. The Mustang's chassis is designed for engaging driving dynamics with a perfect 50/50 weight distribution.

## Safety Features

Despite its performance focus, the Mustang includes modern safety features such as blind-spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, and automatic emergency braking. The vehicle features a comprehensive airbag system and electronic stability control to ensure driver and passenger safety.

## Interior and Comfort

The Mustang's interior combines retro styling with modern technology. The cabin features available leather upholstery, a 12-inch digital cluster, and the latest SYNC infotainment system with wireless connectivity. While not as spacious as sedans, the interior is functional and driver-focused.

## Performance and Handling

The Mustang offers multiple powertrain options, from the base EcoBoost four-cylinder to the high-performance 5.0-liter V8 in GT models. The suspension is tuned for sporty driving with magnetic ride control available on premium trims. Track apps and launch control add to the performance capabilities.

## History and Legacy

Since its introduction in 1964, the Mustang has become an American icon, representing freedom and performance. The seventh-generation Mustang (introduced in 2024) maintains the classic pony car formula while incorporating modern technology and improved efficiency.

## Variants

- EcoBoost: Base model with turbocharged four-cylinder.
- EcoBoost Premium: Enhanced features and styling.
- GT: High-performance V8 variant.
- Mach 1: Special edition with unique styling.
- Dark Horse: Track-focused performance model.

The Ford Mustang continues to evolve while staying true to its heritage, offering enthusiasts a perfect blend of American muscle car tradition and modern performance.</content>
