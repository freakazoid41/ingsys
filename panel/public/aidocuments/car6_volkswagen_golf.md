A practical and refined hatchback that offers Volkswagen's German engineering, advanced technology, and versatile design.

## Detailed Specifications

The Volkswagen Golf is equipped with a 1.4-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine that provides efficient and responsive performance. The engine is mated to an eight-speed automatic transmission that offers smooth operation. The vehicle's MQB platform provides excellent handling and ride comfort.

## Safety Features

The Golf includes Volkswagen's IQ.DRIVE suite with travel assist, front assist with autonomous emergency braking, lane-keeping system, and blind-spot monitoring. The vehicle has earned five-star safety ratings from Euro NCAP and includes comprehensive occupant protection systems.

## Interior and Comfort

The Golf's interior features high-quality materials and modern design with digital displays and the latest infotainment technology. The cabin offers excellent ergonomics with adjustable seating and climate control. The hatchback design provides 15.3 cubic feet of cargo space, expandable to 49.9 cubic feet with rear seats folded.

## Performance and Handling

The Golf delivers precise handling and a comfortable ride quality. The available GTI variant offers significantly more performance with a 2.0-liter turbocharged engine producing 241 horsepower. All-wheel-drive is available on higher trims for enhanced traction.

## History and Legacy

Since its introduction in 1974, the Golf has become one of the best-selling cars in history, with over 37 million units produced. The eighth-generation Golf (introduced in 2020) features modern technology while maintaining the model's core values of practicality and refinement.
