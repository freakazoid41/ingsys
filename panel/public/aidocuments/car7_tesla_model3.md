A revolutionary electric sedan that combines cutting-edge technology, performance, and sustainability in a compelling package.

## Detailed Specifications

The Tesla Model 3 is powered by dual electric motors that provide instant torque and smooth acceleration. The vehicle uses lithium-ion battery technology with over-the-air updates that improve performance and add features. The minimalist design focuses on efficiency and aerodynamics.

## Safety Features

The Model 3 has earned the highest safety rating of any car tested by NHTSA. It features a comprehensive suite of active safety systems including Autopilot, Full Self-Driving capability, automatic emergency braking, and pedestrian detection. The vehicle's rigid structure and low center of gravity contribute to its safety performance.

## Interior and Comfort

The Model 3's interior features a minimalist design with a large 15-inch touchscreen that controls all vehicle functions. The cabin offers premium materials and a spacious feel despite the compact exterior. The frunk and trunk provide 15 cubic feet of cargo space, with additional space under the rear seats.

## Performance and Handling

The Model 3 offers impressive acceleration with 0-60 mph in under 5.8 seconds for the base model. The vehicle's low center of gravity and precise steering provide engaging driving dynamics. Regenerative braking provides excellent energy recovery and one-pedal driving capability.

## History and Legacy

Introduced in 2017, the Model 3 has become Tesla's best-selling vehicle and helped accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles worldwide. The refreshed Model 3 (2024) features improved range, performance, and technology while maintaining the original's revolutionary design.

## Variants

- Standard Range Plus: Base model with 272-mile range.
- Long Range: Extended range up to 358 miles.
- Performance: High-performance variant with 3.1-second 0-60 mph.
- Model 3 Refresh: Updated design and features.
