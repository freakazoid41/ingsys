## Detailed Specifications

The Honda Civic features a turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine that provides ample power for everyday driving while maintaining excellent fuel efficiency. Paired with a continuously variable transmission (CVT), it offers smooth acceleration and a refined driving experience. Its fuel economy ratings make it a top choice for cost-conscious drivers.

## Safety Features

Equipped with Honda Sensing suite, including forward collision warning, lane departure alert, and adaptive cruise control. The Civic also boasts a rigid body structure and multiple airbags for enhanced occupant protection.

## Interior and Comfort

The cabin is spacious for its class, with supportive seats and intuitive controls. The infotainment system supports wireless connectivity and smartphone integration, ensuring a connected driving experience.

## Performance and Handling

With its sport-tuned suspension and precise steering, the Civic delivers engaging dynamics. The available Sport trim adds visual flair and performance enhancements.

## History and Legacy

Since its debut in 1972, the Civic has been a pioneer in fuel-efficient vehicles, evolving through generations to meet modern demands.
