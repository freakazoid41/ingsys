## Detailed Specifications

Powered by a 5.0-liter V8 engine, the Mustang roars with authority, offering exhilarating acceleration. The advanced 10-speed automatic transmission ensures quick shifts, while the aggressive styling turns heads on the road.

## Safety Features

Includes Ford Co-Pilot360 with blind-spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, and automatic emergency braking. The sturdy chassis provides confidence in spirited driving.

## Interior and Comfort

Sporty bucket seats and a driver-focused cockpit enhance the driving experience. Modern infotainment with SYNC 4 keeps you connected.

## Performance and Handling

Exceptional handling with magnetic ride control and Brembo brakes. Track-ready capabilities make it a favorite for performance driving.

## History and Legacy

Launched in 1964, the Mustang has symbolized freedom and power, influencing automotive culture worldwide.
