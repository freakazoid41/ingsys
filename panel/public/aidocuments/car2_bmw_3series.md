A premium compact executive car known for its sporty handling, luxury interior, and advanced technology.

## Detailed Specifications

The BMW 3 Series features a turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine that provides excellent power delivery and efficiency. The engine is mated to an eight-speed automatic transmission that offers quick and smooth shifts. The vehicle's suspension system is tuned for both comfort and sporty driving dynamics, making it versatile for various road conditions.

## Safety Features

The 3 Series comes equipped with BMW's advanced safety systems, including forward collision warning, automatic emergency braking, lane departure warning, and blind-spot monitoring. The vehicle also features a comprehensive airbag system and stability control to protect occupants in various driving scenarios.

## Interior and Comfort

The interior of the BMW 3 Series offers premium materials and modern design. The cabin features high-quality leather upholstery, a digital instrument cluster, and the latest iDrive infotainment system with wireless smartphone integration. Rear seat space is adequate for adults, and cargo capacity is 17 cubic feet.

## Performance and Handling

The 3 Series delivers engaging driving characteristics with precise steering and responsive handling. The M340i variant offers even more performance with a 3.0-liter turbocharged inline-six engine producing 375 horsepower. BMW's xDrive all-wheel-drive system is available for enhanced traction in adverse weather conditions.

## History and Legacy

Since its introduction in 1975, the 3 Series has defined the premium compact car segment. Each generation has pushed the boundaries of design, technology, and performance. The G20 generation (2020-present) continues this tradition with modern connectivity and autonomous driving features.

## Variants

- 330i: Base model with 2.0L turbo engine.
- 330e: Plug-in hybrid variant.
- M340i: High-performance model with 3.0L engine.
- 330d: Diesel option for European markets.
